# told. approved wordmark

Approved for the first app version. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Asset: [transparent PNG](told-wordmark-v1.png). Preserve proportions and the final dot; do not recreate it with a font. A light-on-dark variant and vector master remain production preparation tasks.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: custom wordmark logo, transparent PNG.
Create a professionally drawn bespoke lowercase wordmark spelling exactly "told." including the final period. Solid near-black ink (#171717) on a genuinely transparent background. Only the wordmark, no presentation board, no subtitle, no mockup.
Art direction: sculptural, quietly playful, sophisticated editorial identity for an illustrated audio news app. This must look like original custom lettering, not text typed in a stock font. Soft but confident substantial strokes, compact slightly wide letter proportions, carefully balanced optical spacing, crisp smooth outlines. A distinctive lowercase t with a restrained curved foot and thoughtfully shaped crossbar; slightly oval o with a subtly asymmetric inner counter; lowercase l with a small related curved foot; d bowl echoes the o with an elegant ascender. Make all four letters feel from one coherent custom alphabet. A substantial circular full stop, a little larger than an ordinary font period, placed deliberately close enough to belong to the wordmark. Keep clear separation and legibility between letters. Restrained personality, no gimmicky ligatures.
Composition: one horizontal wordmark centered with generous transparent padding, large clean high-resolution lettering suitable for a brand asset.
Constraints: exact text told. all lowercase; flat monochrome; no gradients, shadows, bevels, textures, illustration, speech bubble, microphone, sparkles, border, extra marks, or background. True alpha transparency, not a checkerboard painted into the image.
